Open in a new tab

The layout engine is jubarte 0.20.0, compiled to WebAssembly. It is downloaded once from cdn.jsdelivr.net (11 MB); your document is converted in this tab and is not sent anywhere.

jubarte targets Word's layout and is scored against Word page by page on 808 documents. A dense document can still differ by a line or a page. Fonts Word would take from your computer are replaced by metric-compatible ones. Not affiliated with Microsoft.
